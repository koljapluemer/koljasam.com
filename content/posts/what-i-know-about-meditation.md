> Even the most seasoned meditator continues to practice seated meditation, because it tunes and sharpens the basic mental skills he needs for his particular game. We must never forget, however, that seated meditation itself is not the game. It’s the practice. The game in which those basic skills are to be applied is the rest of one’s experiential existence. Meditation that is not applied to daily living is sterile and limited.

> *Somewhere in this process, you will come face to face with the sudden and shocking realization that you are completely crazy*. Your mind is a shrieking, gibbering madhouse on wheels barreling pell-mell down the hill, utterly out of control and helpless. No problem. You are not crazier than you were yesterday. It has always been this way, and you just never noticed.

— *📖 Mindfulness in Plain English*

> Meditation that is successful only when you are withdrawn in some soundproof ivory tower is still undeveloped.
